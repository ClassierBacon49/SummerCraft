# SummerCraft

My Summer Car-style car building and driving for Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge).

## Building a car

1. **Place a Car Body** on the ground (or `/summercraft car` for a finished one, `/summercraft body` for a bare one).
2. **Fit parts**: right-click the car holding a part. Blue ghosts show where it can go.
   The engine goes together in order:
   `Engine Block → Crankshaft → Pistons → Cylinder Head → Spark Plugs + Carburetor`,
   with the `Distributor` and `Clutch → Gearbox → Starter Motor` on the block and the `Oil Pan` under the crank.
   The car also needs a Battery, Fuel Tank, Radiator, Exhaust, Brake Pads (front and rear), 4 Wheels,
   seats and a Steering Wheel. Hood, trunk lid, doors, fenders and bumpers bolt on too.
3. **Bolt everything down.** Every bolt has a size (8–16mm, shown when you aim at a part and in the tooltip).
   Use the **Wrench** in the air to change socket (sneak for smaller). Aim at a bolt head and hold right-click
   to tighten, or sneak to loosen. Loose bolts stick out of the part.
4. **Fill fluids**: Motor Oil → oil pan, Jerry Can → fuel tank, Water Bucket → radiator.
5. **Open the door** (empty-hand right-click), get in, and **hold V** to crank the engine.

Hoods, doors and the trunk open and close with an empty-hand right-click. You can't reach the engine with the hood
shut, or the brakes with the wheels on. Paint the car by right-clicking it with any dye.

## Engines

| Engine | Cylinders | Torque | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inline-4 | 4 | 110 Nm | One head, plugs on top |
| Boxer-4 | 4 | 150 Nm | Flat; a head and plug set on each side |
| V6 | 6 | 230 Nm | Two heads |
| V8 | 8 | 340 Nm | Two heads, thirsty |

Add a **Turbocharger** (lag, then big boost, blow-off valve when you lift) or a **Supercharger**
(instant boost and whine), or both. Boost makes heat and knock, so retard the ignition timing.

**Engine Stand:** place it and build an engine on it with easy access from every side. To move an engine,
loosen the engine block's bolts and sneak + empty-hand right-click the block: the whole engine (with clutch,
gearbox, and every bolt and setting) comes off as one item. Right-click an empty engine bay or stand to set it down.
This also works for pulling an engine out of a car.

**Dyno Roller:** place two side by side, drive the rear wheels onto them and do a full-throttle pull in 3rd or 4th.
The car stays put and a graph of torque and horsepower against RPM appears top-right.

`/summercraft car <engine> <boost>` and `/summercraft stand <engine> <boost>` spawn finished ones, for example
`/summercraft car v8 twin`. Engines: `inline_4`, `boxer_4`, `v6`, `v8`. Boost: `none`, `turbo`, `supercharger`, `twin`.

## Driving

It's a manual, rear-wheel-drive car with a clutch.

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| W / S | Throttle / brake |
| A / D | Steer |
| Space | Handbrake |
| Left Ctrl | Clutch (hold) |
| ↑ / ↓ | Shift up / down (needs the clutch) |
| V | Ignition (hold to crank, tap to switch off) |
| H | Horn |

Pull away by holding the clutch, shifting into 1st, adding some throttle and letting go. Dropping the clutch with no
revs, or pulling away in a high gear, stalls it. Shifting without the clutch grinds.
The tyres have real grip limits: too much throttle spins the rear wheels, too much speed into a corner slides,
and the handbrake swings the back out. Ice, snow, sand, gravel and dirt all have less grip than stone or concrete.

## Tuning and wear

Use the **Screwdriver** on the carburetor (air/fuel mixture) and distributor (ignition timing). Sneak to turn the
other way. A new carb runs rich and a new distributor is retarded, so tuning both gives noticeably more power.
- Too rich: black smoke, high fuel use, fouled plugs, backfires.
- Too lean or too retarded: the engine runs hot. Too advanced: knock, which wears the pistons.
- Way off: it won't start.

Parts wear out (shown as durability):
- **Tyres** wear from distance and from sliding.
- **Clutch** wears from slipping.
- **Brakes** wear from use.
- **Spark plugs** wear from running time.
- **Pistons** wear from running hot, without oil, or with knock.

Swap in fresh parts to fix them.

## Things that go wrong

- Loose engine parts: it cranks but won't start. Loose battery or starter: *click.*
- Loose parts shake off at speed and take whatever is mounted on them along. Loose oil pan, tank or radiator leaks.
- A hard crash knocks bumpers, fenders and the hood loose and can crack the radiator.
- No coolant makes the engine overheat (watch the TEMP gauge). Out of oil or too hot means it seizes.

## Development

```
gradlew runClient                      # dev client
gradlew runClient -PquickPlay="World"  # straight into a save
gradlew build                          # jar in build/libs/
```

Textures and sounds are generated by `tools/gen_textures.py` and `tools/gen_sounds.py`
(`py -m venv tools/.venv`, then `tools/.venv/Scripts/pip install pillow numpy soundfile`).
Setting `SUMMERCRAFT_SHOWCASE=1` makes the client spawn a car, photograph it from several angles into
`run/screenshots/`, and quit.

Requires JDK 21.
